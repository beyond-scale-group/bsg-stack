## Script bug found during reconciliation (step 1)

`reconcile-milestones.sh` printed `0 epics parsed from plan` even
though `po/PLAN.md` has 10 well-formed `## Epics` bindings. Root
cause: it re-derives the milestone slug via a jq regex `capture()`
against `.raw`, but `parse-plan.sh` already strips `[milestone:...]`
brackets out of `raw` into `.bindings.milestones`. Worse, the jq
pattern `EXPR as $var` where `EXPR` can evaluate to `empty` silently
drops the whole per-epic pipeline output, so even the inferred-title
fallback never runs. **This script has never actually assigned a
milestone or cleared `scope-creep` via plan bindings since it was
introduced (#288/e77a879).** Filed as
[#657](https://github.com/beyond-scale-group/bsg-stack/issues/657)
(→ tech, milestone E10-label-simplification, `needs:tech`).
